The month in short

July was a genuinely better month on both halves of the account, and the single most useful thing we did was stop trusting the enquiry count.

The advertising spent $1,476.08, down from $2,927.48 in June, and produced 25 tracked enquiries. On the raw numbers that is $61.50 an enquiry, less than half of June. But we then listened to every call and read every form individually. Of the 21 July paid enquiries we have judged so far, 8 were real, workable jobs. That is $184.51 per real job, and it is the number we will hold the advertising to from here, because an enquiry count can be flattered by wrong numbers and a real job cannot.

It also let us keep chipping away at the long running problem on this account: the share of the budget going to people looking for their town's sanitation department, or trying to reach a competitor by name. They ring, realise they have the wrong company, and hang up, and Google counts each one as a conversion. We have been tightening this progressively, and July's review let us take the biggest single step yet. There is a section on it below, including what it means for next month's numbers.

Organic search had its best month of the year. Clicks more than doubled, 11 to 24, impressions rose 28% to 5,480, and the three month decline reversed. The site is still ranking too deep to convert that visibility into traffic at any scale, and we explain exactly where the ceiling is.

Your Google Business Profile was shown 60% more often than in June. It is also, on the evidence we gathered this month, the biggest single opportunity you have, and the fix is mostly in your hands rather than ours.

Where the wrong number calls are still coming from, and the latest round of blocks

This is the part of the account we have been working at longest. Demolition and waste sit next to each other in Google's eyes, so the ads keep being offered to people who want a bin emptied rather than a building taken down, and each round of blocks buys back a little more of the budget.

Measuring it again across 8 June to 6 August: of the $1,727 of spend Google itemises, roughly $933, or 54%, still went to searches that were never going to become a demolition job.

They fall into two groups. People searching for a municipal service: town of greenburgh sanitation, new rochelle sanitation phone number, yonkers sanitation phone number. And people searching for a competitor by name: royal carting, casella, win waste, interstate waste, cherry hill dumpsters. The call recordings confirm it. One caller wanted to know where to dispose of a printer and a mattress, another was reporting a broken traffic light, another was chasing a missed garbage collection.

On 6 August we added a further 43 blocks, the widest set we have applied to this account so far, built from the call recordings rather than guesswork. We tested the list against 1,414 past searches first: every genuine demolition search it would have caught had never cost a cent, so nothing of value is being blocked.

Please expect the conversion number to fall next month, and do not read it as a decline

Those wrong numbers were being counted as conversions, because the caller pressed the call button on the ad. 24 of roughly 63 recorded conversions across the last two months were calls of that kind. Now that they are blocked, the conversion count in any Google report will drop by something like a third.

That fall is the fix working, not the account getting worse. The number that should hold, and the one we will report, is real jobs and what each one costs.

The ceiling: your home page is competing with your own service pages

The site is shown 5,480 times a month and earns 24 clicks from it, because almost everything ranks on page three or worse. The reason is specific and fixable.

Your home page absorbs 57% of all impressions, and Google keeps choosing it ahead of the pages built for each service. For the search demolition westchester ny, your home page sits at position 13 while your actual demolition services page sits at position 52. That services page earned zero clicks all month. Your About and Contact pages are shown more often than it is.

Across the month, 111 of your 397 search terms were split across two or more pages of your own site. One search alone, demolition westchester ny, was answered by thirteen different pages. When Google cannot tell which page is the right answer, it defaults to the home page, and a home page ranks worse for a specific job than a page about that job.

On 6 August we started fixing this by linking your Westchester page directly into the demolition, interior demolition and commercial demolition pages using the words people actually search for. That page is your second most visible page and it previously linked to none of them.

Fifteen searches sitting just off page one

There is a cluster of "near me" searches ranked between position 16 and 28, each shown 23 to 56 times a month, and earning no clicks at all, because almost nobody scrolls that far. demolition services near me, demolition contractors near me, demolition company near me, home demolition near me and eleven more like them.

They are moving in the right direction on their own. home demolition near me improved from position 43.5 to 20.2 in a single month, and residential demolition companies near me from 87.6 to 50.3. Getting this group onto page one is the clearest available win in organic search, and it is the focus for August.

You do not appear in the map results for any of your main services

We checked the Google results for your ten most commercially important searches, measured specifically within Westchester County. Demolition Experts NY did not appear in the top three map results for a single one of them.

Those three map slots sit above the normal results and take a large share of the calls. In your county they are held by:

  • Delta Demolition Westchester, in the map results for 7 of the 10 searches, rated 5.0 from 181 reviews
  • Westchester Demolition Pros, also 7 of 10, rated 5.0 from 20 reviews
  • Westchester Demo and Junk Removal, 3 of 10, rated 5.0 from 11 reviews

The pattern is hard to miss. The business holding the most map placements has 181 reviews, and every other local competitor sits between 11 and 55. Review count is the most visible difference between them and you.

This is the highest value thing available to your business right now, and it needs you rather than us. A steady flow of reviews from completed jobs, asked for at the point the work is finished, would do more for your enquiry volume than any change we can make to the website or the ads. If it would help, we will write the request message and set up a process for sending it.
